## Shot List

### 0:00–0:18 — The Pain (Maya)
- Visual: phone photo of a highlighted textbook; a red "54" on an exam paper
- VO: *"Maya studies harder than anyone in her class. She read this chapter four times. She scored a fifty-four. And when her mom asked what happened, she said the nine words every student knows: 'I knew it yesterday. It just… went away.'"*

### 0:18–0:35 — The Revelation
- Visual: animated forgetting curve plunging; text "70% forgotten in 24 hours — known since 1885"
- VO: *"Science has known about the forgetting curve for 140 years. Highlighting doesn't fight it. Re-reading doesn't. Maya was never bad at studying — studying was failing her."*

### 0:35–1:00 — The Magic (product, part 1)
- Visual: photograph chapter → processing screen with **live agent pipeline** lighting up (Ingestion → Concepts → three parallel generators) → latency counter → explainer appears with a football analogy (her DNA)
- Split-screen beat: same concept, cricket-analogy version vs cooking-analogy version for a different learner
- VO: *"Mastery Engine turns any page into a full study system in sixty seconds — written the way each brain learns. It asked Maya how she learns first."*

### 1:00–1:25 — The Proof (adaptive loop ⭐)
- Visual: quiz → wrong answer ×2 → Remediation Coach chat re-teaches via analogy → mind-map node ripples **red→amber→green**
- VO: *"And when Maya forgets something — because everyone does — it already knows. Her Coach re-teaches it a different way until it clicks, and remembers which way worked… for her."*

### 1:25–1:45 — Breadth & Credibility
- Visual: fast montage — flashcards w/ hints, daily review queue + streak, audio lesson, teacher worksheets, YouTube-link upload
- VO: *"Quizzes, flashcards, audio lessons, review sessions scheduled by real spaced-repetition science — and teachers can turn one lesson into materials for every reading level. Built on a multi-agent LangGraph system."*

### 1:45–2:00 — The Payoff
- Visual: exam paper "87" → Maya smiles → logo + tagline + live URL
- VO: *"Every student studies hard. Mastery Engine makes sure hard work finally pays off. Remember everything. mastery-engine.vercel.app"*
