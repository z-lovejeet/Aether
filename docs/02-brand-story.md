# 02 · Brand & Story Bible
## Mastery Engine

---

## 1. The Core Human Truth
> **You don't fail exams because you didn't study. You fail because nobody ever taught you how to *remember*.**

In 1885, Hermann Ebbinghaus discovered the Forgetting Curve: within 24 hours you lose up to 70% of what you learned; within a week, 90%. Known for 140 years — and almost no tool built for students actually fights it.

**The villain:** not laziness, not bad teachers — the Forgetting Curve itself.
**The hero:** Mastery Engine.

## 2. The Character: Maya, 15
> Maya is not a weak student. She makes colorful notes, highlights her textbook in three colors, watches explainers at 2× speed. She *cares*.
>
> Last month she studied biology four times for her unit test. She walked into the exam hall knowing the material. She walked out with a 54.
>
> Her mother asked, "But you studied so much — what happened?" And Maya said the sentence millions of students say every day:
>
> ### *"I knew it yesterday. It just... went away."*
>
> Nobody told Maya about the forgetting curve. She wasn't failing at studying. **Studying was failing her.**

## 3. The Turning Point
> Then Maya photographs her chapter before the next test. Sixty seconds later, Mastery Engine knows her — she loves football, learns through stories, has twenty minutes a night. It builds her a system. When she starts forgetting the Calvin cycle — and she will, because everyone does — the system already knows, *before she does*, and brings it back at exactly the right moment, taught a different way.
>
> She walks into the exam and nothing feels missing. She gets an 87. But the number isn't the point.

## 4. Brand Assets
- **Name:** Mastery Engine
- **Tagline (primary):** "Remember Everything."
- **Alternates:** "Study once. Remember forever." · "Your brain forgets. We don't let it."
- **Mission line:** We fight the 140-year-old forgetting curve — so no student ever says "I knew it yesterday" again.

## 5. Tone of Voice
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Warm, confident, human | Corporate, robotic |
| Name the pain precisely ("I knew it yesterday") | Guilt-trip students about effort |
| Science-backed claims (Ebbinghaus, retrieval practice) | Hype words ("revolutionary AI") |
| Short punchy sentences on landing | Wall-of-text features lists |

## 6. Messaging Per Audience
- **Students:** "Stop re-reading. Start remembering."
- **Parents:** "Your child works hard. This makes the work stick."
- **Teachers:** "One lesson in. Every reading level out."
- **Judges/devs:** "A LangGraph multi-agent system that personalizes generation AND runs an adaptive remediation feedback loop on top of SM-2 spaced repetition."

## 7. Emotional Arc (used across video, landing, Devpost)
1. **Empathy** — Maya fails despite trying
2. **Revelation** — the forgetting curve; 70% gone in 24 h
3. **Magic** — photograph → personalized system in 60 s
4. **Proof** — mind-map node blooms green; the 87
5. **Meaning** — *"Every student studies hard. We make sure hard work finally pays off."*
